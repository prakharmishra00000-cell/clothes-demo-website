Our Heritage

Bespoke Craftsmanship & Sustainable Threads

"We design garments that outlive trends—weaving details that connect modern aesthetics to historical roots."

Founded in 2024, Vogue & Co. began with a humble workspace and a single loom. Today, we stand as a beacon of slow fashion, focusing on organic material sourcing, ethical weaving processes, and premium clothing that feels like a second skin.

Every stitch is crafted by master artisans who pour years of experience and love into custom blazers, soft linen shirts, and intricate silk sarees. We believe luxury is an experience, not a price tag.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the right size? +

Please check our comprehensive size guide listed inside each product's details page. Alternatively, our bespoke sizing experts can guide you on custom fittings via WhatsApp.

What payment methods are accepted? +

We support order inquiries via WhatsApp, bank transfers, credit/debit card invoices, and standard secure online gateways upon design finalization.

How long does delivery take? +

Standard in-stock clothing is shipped within 24-48 hours. Custom orders and tailored blazers require 7-10 business days for detailed assembly and packaging.

Are the products authentic? +

Yes, all Vogue & Co. apparel is designed and handcrafted in our local ateliers. Each item comes with an authenticity guarantee tag containing the artisan's signature.

Do you offer exchanges? +

We offer a 14-day hassle-free exchange window for all standard apparel in unworn condition. Tailored garments are eligible for free sizing adjustments.
